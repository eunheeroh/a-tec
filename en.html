Convergence of AI Education & Digital Publishing

A-Tec Lab is a specialized research institute founded on four core values: AI (Artificial Intelligence), Teach (Education), e-Publishing (Digital Publishing), and Contents.

We are building a world where anyone can learn easily and publish freely. By developing AI-powered educational content and digital publishing solutions, we innovate how knowledge is created and delivered.
